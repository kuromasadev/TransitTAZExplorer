About the data and proximity method

Neighborhoods are the project’s draft O-D areas, assembled from 1,239 TAZs. Region sectors use the project’s H-GAC assignments, with sector 11 subdivisions combined. Corridor suggestions use each TAZ’s nearest active commuter facility; they are proximity groupings, not official catchments. A neighborhood can appear in more than one sector or corridor.

Facility ranking uses great-circle straight-line miles from the area-weighted center of the selected neighborhood’s TAZs in the selected sector/corridor, or from the selected TAZ center. It is not distance from a home address or a road route. Facilities are ranked across all corridors so the closest one is not hidden by a corridor choice. Driving-time catchments can replace this method later.

Routes and facility stops checked against METRO’s official schedule pages on October 12, 2026. The app is an offline snapshot. Confirm current service before travel. General destination areas describe direct route service; exact stops and departures may differ by direction or trip. Facility addresses and geometry retain the project inventory vintage.
